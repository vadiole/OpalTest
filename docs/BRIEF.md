# Brief

Users earn rewards as their successful referrals increase. The program has multiple reward tiers, and users claim rewards once eligible. It supports both Free and Pro users; the free year of Opal Pro is available only to Free users.

Users should be able to share their referral link in order to earn rewards. You should use mock data; no real backend is required but consider how you would easily connect to a real backend later.

This brief intentionally leaves some decisions open. Approach it as you would a real piece of work with our team.
